# Business insights report template

After running the project, replace bracketed fields with measured results from `outputs/kpi_summary.json`, charts and SQL queries.

## Executive summary
- Net revenue: ₹[value]
- Net profit: ₹[value]
- Profit margin: [value]%
- Unique customers: [value]
- Order count: [value]

## Findings and actions
| Area | Evidence to extract | Business action |
|---|---|---|
| Trend | Strongest/weakest revenue month | Plan inventory and campaigns around demand |
| Category | Highest revenue and profit categories | Protect margin in profitable categories |
| Region | Revenue and profit by region | Allocate stock and marketing by contribution |
| Discounts | Margin by discount band | Avoid discount levels that destroy contribution |
| Returns | Return rate by category/product | Investigate quality, sizing or fulfillment |
| Customers | RFM segment counts and value | Retain champions; reactivate at-risk buyers |

Do not claim a category or region is best until you have checked the generated outputs.
